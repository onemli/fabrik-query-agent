Policy Control Enforcement Direction. It is used for defining policy enforcemnt direction for the traffic coming to or from an L3Out. Egress and Ingress directions are wrt L3Out. This also means that there is no special upgrade sequence needed for upgrading to the release introducing this feature. After upgrade user would have to change the property value to Ingress.
