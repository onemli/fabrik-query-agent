statsDestP

Stats Destination · stats:DestP

The export policy. The export policy enables you to output statistics collection policy reports in an XML or JSON format. The reports are exported at a specified frequency and to a chosen export destination. Specifying the export destination can require selecting the protocol used when communicating with a remote server and entering a hostname/IP address, a remote path, the username and password the system should use to log in to the remote server, and the distinguished name for a relation to the endpoint group used by the remote host.
