bgpRtExpP

Route Export Policy · bgp:RtExpP

Route export policy to control whether to export routes into a different address family. Destination address family is specified in the object. Object may be nested within peer Address family (AF) to subject only those peer's particular AF routes to export. Object can also be under a domain Address family in which case it is applicable to all peers and local routes
