vnsAbsGraph

L4-L7 Service Graph Template · vns:AbsGraph

The abstract graph is made up of abstract nodes and used to define the traffic flow through a service function such as load balancing, SSL offload, and firewall. Abstract nodes are comprised of service nodes such as a service node balancer (SLB) or firewall (FW), abstract term nodes (the nodes that are connected to endpoint groups), and connections.
